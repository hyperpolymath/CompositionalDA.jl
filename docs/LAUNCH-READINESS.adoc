== Stage boundaries

* Research/pre-alpha: explicit scope/refusals, reproducible baseline, no unsupported
  calibration/clinical/stable claim. Adverse findings stay visible.
* Alpha candidate: applicable CRG minimum and canonical repository requirements,
  tested installation and user workflow, signed provenance, known-bug treatment,
  and human approval of the exact bounded claim. Test count alone is insufficient.
* Beta candidate: independent reproduction and scientific review; adequately
  powered, prespecified calibration; relevant external datasets/targets; mutation,
  fuzz and seam campaigns; measured performance/support budgets and regression policy.
* Stable candidate: satisfy the applicable estate V1 gate, including all claimed
  proof/test/benchmark/security obligations and artifact/claim parity. A green CI
  icon or version number cannot substitute for these requirements.

General submission additionally needs correct name/UUID/compat/licence, clean
installation without an unregistered dependency, declared AI assistance and the
human review General requires. Registration review, the waiting period, tagging
and release publication remain distinct events. Other registries need their own
adapters for namespace, licence, provenance, binaries, signing and review rules.

== Risk and uncertainty

Prioritise severity, exposure, detectability and confidence; retain the raw
evidence rather than treating an ordinal risk score as a probability. Calibration
failure outranks cosmetic completeness. Define a Six Sigma defect and opportunity
before collecting DPMO; dependent test assertions are not independent customer
opportunities. No Six Sigma capability is inferred from these tests.

Budget independent datasets, not just taxon-level observations. Record model
misspecification, selection/refusal bias, Monte-Carlo uncertainty, software drift
and human applicability review separately. Each release claim needs an owner,
acceptance criterion fixed before measurement, receipt, expiry/retest trigger,
remaining uncertainty and a rollback/withdrawal decision.

The comprehensive independent-review prompt and 18-by-14 ledger are maintained
in the review packet. They must remain capable of rejecting this assessment.
