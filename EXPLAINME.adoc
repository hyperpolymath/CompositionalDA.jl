= What CompositionalDA can establish

This package analyses raw integer counts in samples-by-features orientation.
Its current methods are unpaired two-group ALDEx2 and original ANCOM-BC.
It does not implement ANCOM-BC2, covariate adjustment or repeated measures.
Zero handling, the contrast direction, seeds and numerical policies belong in
the analysis record. Swapping groups changes the effect direction.

== Evidence and its limits

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Claim |Owning evidence |What remains outside it
|Exact algebraic identities |`proofs/PROOF-STATUS.md`, safe Agda modules and negative controls
|Floating-point refinement, sorter correctness, FDR and biological truth
|Numerical method agreement |`test/oracle/`, pinned R/Bioconductor releases and explicitly repaired reference functions
|Equivalence to pristine defective references, every input or later upstream releases
|Regression resistance |`test/`, seeded fuzz/property tests and planted mutants
|Exhaustive path/state coverage or the absence of unknown defects
|Real processed microbiome inputs |`docs/DATASETS.md`, archived source hashes and gut/soil fixtures
|Known ground truth, prospective clinical validity or general calibration
|Exploratory operating characteristics |`docs/CALIBRATION-PILOT-PROTOCOL.adoc`, `benchmark/calibration_pilot.jl`
|A calibration certificate; the small-unbalanced ANCOM-BC null pilot is adverse
|Performance |`benchmark/benchmarks.jl`, machine-specific raw measurements
|A speed advantage over R or an agreed latency/service-level guarantee
|===

The 2026-11-07 calibration pilot fixed six regimes and 32 independently seeded
datasets per regime before measurement. Of 384 method fits, 365 returned and
19 refused. Rates conditional on returned fits must be accompanied by refusal
counts. Under the small-unbalanced complete null, original ANCOM-BC made false
discoveries in 19/29 successful fits at 0.05 (Wilson 95% interval 0.473–0.801).
The unconditional observed count is 19/32; refusals are not successful negative
findings. The follow-up repaired-R experiment converged for all 32 datasets and
also made false discoveries in 19. Of 29 paired successful native fits, 28 had
maximum q-value differences below 8.5e-6, but replicate 27 differed by 0.5041
and changed the discovery decision. Numerical conformance is therefore not
established across this expanded regime. A reviewed support restriction or
estimator correction, and fresh prespecified validation, are needed before a
calibration claim. The raw receipt is CI run 42411431729, artifact
`small-unbalanced-calibration`, at source `cbb8ac4d85b4259c8122ed83c2cce8ec6c34362a`.
Do not fix it by tuning the pilot seeds, removing adverse regimes or quietly
substituting another method.

ALDEx2's pilot is much less adverse, but 32 datasets cannot certify 5% FDR.
All-null FDR is the probability of any rejection. With signal present,
family-wise error is the probability of at least one false rejection; mean
false discovery proportion estimates FDR. The reported Wilson intervals are
for event probabilities, not mean FDP, taxa treated as independent
replications, or arbitrary populations.

At only two ALDEx2 Monte-Carlo instances, a separate 4,096-seed probe produced
58 infinite and three undefined effect estimates. Its p/q bounds still held.
The WebUI refuses non-finite effects; this is an estimator usability limitation
to address explicitly, not permission to coerce undefined effects to zero.

== Trust and release decisions

Julia, GMP/MPFR, BLAS, dependencies, the operating system, reference software,
data provenance and proof checkers remain in the trusted environment.
Agda algebra does not independently verify those implementations. No second
Rocq/Lean witness or full executable refinement is claimed.

General registration is explicitly on hold pending the human maintainer's review
of the changed implementation. A signature authenticates a commit, not its
mathematical or scientific correctness. Version 0.3.0 in Project.toml is not a
stable-release grade. No CRG/TRG/RSR certificate is asserted.
